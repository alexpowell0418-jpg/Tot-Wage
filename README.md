# Tot Wage — Potato Cathedral

Tot Wage converts dollar prices into symbolic potatoes and tater tots.

## Formula

The working formula comes from a 30 lb box of tater tots costing $150.

- 30 lb = 480 oz
- Estimated 9 tots per ounce = 4,320 tots
- $150 ÷ 4,320 ≈ $0.0347 per tot
- Estimated 40 tots per potato
- 1 symbolic potato ≈ $1.388

So:

```text
symbolic potatoes = dollars ÷ 1.388
symbolic tots = dollars ÷ 0.0347
```

## Potato Cathedral

The point is not strict grocery pricing. It is a playful and serious way to ask how money, labor, food, access, comfort, waste, and abundance remain tied together beneath the Veil of Plenty.

“How much embodied life can one hour of labor access?”
