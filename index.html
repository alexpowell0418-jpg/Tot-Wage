Potato Cathedral

Tot Wage converts dollar prices into symbolic potatoes and tater tots.

The logistics visualization scales upward:

  • 🥔 loose potatoes
  • 🥔👜 20 lb bags
  • 📦 pallets
  • 🚚 trucks
  • 🚢 ships

The point is not strict grocery pricing. It is a playful and serious way to visualize labor, scale, infrastructure, and abundance beneath the Veil of Plenty.
